Understanding UNSPSC

Structure & Hierarchy

UNSPSC uses a hierarchical 8-digit code structure divided into four levels:

Level Digits Example
Segment XX000000 43 (IT & Broadcasting)
Family XXYY0000 43.21 (Computer Equipment)
Class XXYYZZ00 43.21.16 (Computer accessories)
Commodity XXYYZZKK 43.21.16.07 (Computer speakers)

Industry Applications

UNSPSC is widely used across these sectors:

  • • E-procurement platforms and marketplaces
  • • Supply chain management systems
  • • Spend analysis and vendor management
  • • Government procurement (GSA schedules)
  • • Healthcare supply chain optimization

Version History & Updates

UNSPSC is maintained by GS1 US and updated regularly. The current version (v26.0801 from August 2023) includes over 150000 active codes covering products and services across all industries.

ETIM Technical Classification Guide

ETIM Structure Explained

ETIM uses alphanumeric class codes with descriptive attributes:

  • Class Code: Format like EC000123 (Electrical)
  • Group Structure: Technical, Electrical, Climate control
  • Attributes: Technical specs, dimensions, materials
  • Values: Standardized measurement units

Regional Variations

Different ETIM standards serve specific markets:

  • • ETIM International: Global standard (English)
  • • ETIM Germany: German market focus
  • • ETIM US: North American adaptation
  • • ETIM Nordic: Scandinavian markets

Technical Product Focus

ETIM specializes in technical and electrical products including circuit breakers, HVAC systems, lighting fixtures, cables, and industrial automation equipment. Each class includes detailed technical specifications and performance characteristics.

NAICS Business Classification System

NAICS vs SIC Comparison

Key differences from the older Standard Industrial Classification:

  • • 6-digit codes vs 4-digit SIC codes
  • • Service sector emphasis (vs manufacturing focus)
  • • North American trilateral cooperation (US/Canada/Mexico)
  • • Updated every 5 years (vs SIC 1987 final update)
  • • Better alignment with modern economy

Government Contracting

NAICS codes are essential for:

  • • Federal contract opportunities (SAM.gov)
  • • Small business size standards determination
  • • SBA certification programs (8(a), HUBZone, etc)
  • • Statistical reporting and tax purposes
  • • Industry benchmarking and analysis

Code Structure & Hierarchy

NAICS uses a hierarchical structure from 2-digit sectors to 6-digit national industries. The 2022 revision includes 1170 industries across 20 sectors, updated every 5 years to reflect economic changes.

ISIC: UN's Global Economic Classification

UN Statistics Division Role

Developed and maintained by the United Nations Statistics Division:

  • • Global standard since 1948
  • • Used by 200+ countries and territories
  • • Foundation for national accounts compilation
  • • International comparability standard
  • • Regular 10-year revision cycles

International Comparability

ISIC enables cross-country economic analysis:

  • • GDP comparison across nations
  • • Employment statistics alignment
  • • Trade flow analysis
  • • Development indicator measurement
  • • Policy impact assessment

ISIC vs NAICS Comparison

While ISIC provides global standardization, NAICS offers more detailed industry breakdowns for North America. ISIC Rev.4 has 21 sections compared to NAICS 20 sectors, with ISIC focusing on international comparability versus NAICS regional specificity.

Harmonized System (HS) Code Guide

Customs & Trade Applications

HS codes are essential for international trade:

  • • Customs duty calculation and tariff schedules
  • • Import/export licensing requirements
  • • Trade statistics and economic analysis
  • • Anti-dumping and safeguard measures
  • • Free trade agreement qualification

Global Implementation

Used by customs authorities worldwide:

  • • 200+ countries and territories
  • • World Customs Organization (WCO) standard
  • • 6-digit base with national extensions
  • • Updated every 5-6 years (2022 latest)
  • • 5000+ commodity groups

Structure & Classification

HS uses a hierarchical structure: 21 Sections → 99 Chapters → 1,000+ Headings → 5000+ Subheadings. Each 6-digit code represents a specific commodity, with countries adding 2-4 digit national extensions for detailed tariff lines.

{% endif %} {% if classifier_type == 'CN' %}

Combined Nomenclature (CN) Code Guide

EU Customs & Trade Applications

CN codes are essential for European Union trade:

  • • EU customs declarations and tariff schedules
  • • Import/export duty calculation within EU
  • • Intrastat reporting for intra-EU trade
  • • Common Customs Tariff (CCT) compliance
  • • TARIC system integration

Structure & Hierarchy

CN extends HS codes with EU-specific details:

  • • 8-digit EU extension of 6-digit HS codes
  • • Annual updates aligned with EU trade policy
  • • Chapter (2 digits), heading (4 digits), subheading (6 digits)
  • • CN subheadings (8 digits) for EU-specific needs
  • • TARIC codes (10 digits) for additional measures

European Union Implementation

CN is mandatory for all EU member states and serves as the legal basis for customs tariffs and trade statistics across the European Union. It provides the common language for customs procedures and ensures consistent classification throughout the single market.

NACE: EU Economic Activity Classification

EU Statistical Framework

NACE is the statistical classification of economic activities in the EU:

  • • European statistical system foundation
  • • National accounts and GDP calculations
  • • Business registers and company surveys
  • • Employment and labor market statistics
  • • Structural business statistics (SBS)

Harmonized EU Standards

NACE ensures consistency across EU member states:

  • • Compatible with ISIC Rev.4 international standard
  • • 4-level hierarchical structure (21 sections)
  • • Regular updates every 5-10 years
  • • Multilingual implementation across EU
  • • Cross-country economic comparisons

Business & Policy Applications

NACE codes are used by Eurostat, national statistical institutes, and EU policymakers for economic analysis, regional development planning, and monitoring of economic performance across different sectors and regions.

{% endif %} {% if classifier_type == 'CPV' %}

CPV: Common Procurement Vocabulary Guide

EU Public Procurement

CPV is essential for EU public procurement:

  • • EU public procurement directives
  • • TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) system
  • • e-Procurement platforms across EU
  • • Contract award notices and tender documents
  • • Procurement statistics and market analysis

Structure & Classification

CPV provides comprehensive procurement categories:

  • • 9-digit hierarchical code structure
  • • Main vocabulary with 45,000+ categories
  • • Supplementary vocabulary for specialized needs
  • • Divisions, groups, classes, and categories
  • • Multilingual EU-wide standardization

Transparency & Competition

CPV enables transparent and competitive public procurement across the European Union, facilitating cross-border tendering and ensuring equal access to public contracts for all EU businesses.

NSN: NATO Stock Number Classification Guide

Structure & Format

NSN uses a standardized 13-digit identification format:

  • • 4-digit FSC: Federal Supply Class (e.g. 1005 - Guns, 75mm through 125mm)
  • • 2-digit NCB: NATO Country Code (e.g. 00-09 for USA, 99 for UK)
  • • 7-digit NIIN: National Item Identification Number
  • • Complete NSN: 1234-00-123-4567 format
  • • Unique identification: Every item has one NSN globally

NATO & Military Applications

NSN is essential for defense and military logistics:

  • • NATO standardization agreements (STANAGs)
  • • Military supply chain management
  • • Defense procurement and contracting
  • • Allied interoperability programs
  • • Weapon systems and equipment tracking

Global Standardization

NSN enables seamless logistics across NATO member nations and partner countries. The system provides universal identification for over 6 million items, facilitating joint operations, equipment sharing, and multinational defense procurement while maintaining supply chain visibility and inventory control.
